5. What we have assumed and do not yet know

The numbers stand or fall on a handful of assumptions Dexter and Siva should challenge directly.

  • India day rates assume freelance/small-studio rates we can hire and hold. With an agency or Singapore contractors, the software line roughly triples. Test this first, with real candidate rates.
  • Platform ops team at S$4,000/month is the largest recurring line and the least validated. It depends on contracted versus hired build and the support load 500 devices generate.
  • Device life of 24 months rests on the recycling model (R-007); the field trial is what proves it. Units dying at 12 months doubles amortisation.
  • Query volume of ~200 per device per month is a guess; the constrained scope (C-010) caps the damage. The CIS pilot will measure the real number.
  • Retention positions are provisional (A-010). The storage line is small either way — the compliance cost of getting it wrong is not.
  • CIS pricing is unresolved. Design-partner pricing moves break-even right and shifts recovery onto external agencies. Decide before the pilot, not after.
  • The retainer is a placeholder and the one-off totals exclude it. A number is needed in that line before this estimate means anything to a bank account.
